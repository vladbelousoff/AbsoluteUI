# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Player, target and focus frames with 3D portraits, shields, a recent-damage
  indicator, low health warning and rarity accents.
- Compact target-of-target cards for target and focus.
- Target and focus auras with debuff type borders and short countdowns.
- Restyled player, target and focus castbars with a latency marker.
- Restyled action bars, bag bar and micro menu, with options to hide the
  micro menu and bag bar.
- Skins for tooltips, the quest tracker, the loot window, bags (including
  Bagnon) and the damage meter.
- Bar texture and bar font settings.
- Settings panel with an overview page and one page per part of the UI.
