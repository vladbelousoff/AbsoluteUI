# Changelog

## 1.5.0

- Character window skin, Forever and TBC. Gear slots get item quality
  borders like the bags. Toggle under Skins.

## 1.4.0

- TBC Anniversary support. Same zip works for TBC and Forever.
- On TBC pretty much everything is skinned: unit frames, castbars, action
  bars, bags, tooltips, menus, quest windows, spellbook/talents, map and quest
  log. The micro menu buttons stay Blizzard's for now.
- "Value and percent" health text is now "9.4k (85%)". The old separator
  sometimes showed a stray "|r".
- Pet buffs no longer overlap the bottom of the pet frame.

## 1.3.0

- Your castbar always shows the spell icon now, not only when it's attached to
  the player frame.
- XP and rep bars get the castbar look (purple XP, blue while rested, rep
  colored by standing). Can be turned off under Action Bars.
- Bevel is the new default bar texture. If you already picked one it stays.

## 1.2.0

- New skin for the world map and quest log. Toggle under Skins.
- The +, - and x on window buttons are drawn now instead of using font
  glyphs, so they're actually centered.
- Honor, skill and rep rewards in the quest window get the item card look too.

## 1.1.1

- Now on CurseForge as well. No addon changes.

## 1.1.0

- New skins: spellbook and talents, right-click/dropdown menus, NPC quest and
  gossip windows.
- Pet frame gets the same small card as target of target. The border goes red
  while your pet is attacking.
- Swing timers use the castbar look.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Player, target and focus frames with 3D portraits, absorbs, damage trail,
  low health warning and elite/rare markers
- Target of target cards
- Target/focus auras with debuff colors and short timers
- Castbars with latency
- Action bars, bag bar and micro menu (bags and micro menu can be hidden)
- Skins for tooltips, quest tracker, loot, bags (Bagnon too) and the damage
  meter
- Bar texture and font options
