# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- Your castbar shows the spell icon wherever Edit Mode places it, not only
  when attached to the player frame.
- Experience and reputation bars get the castbar look: a flat bar in a box,
  purple experience (blue while rested, with rested experience shaded past
  the fill) and reputation colored by standing. Toggle it under Action Bars.
- Bevel is the default bar texture. Settings you already saved keep their
  texture.

## 1.2.0

- Skin for the world map and the quest log beside it: flat panel, a
  breadcrumb navigation bar with the current zone in gold, flat map buttons
  (waypoint pin, floor picker, filter, quest log toggle), zone headers as
  tiles, flat track checkboxes, thin scroll bars and a quest count tile.
  Quest details get light text and reward cards with quality borders.
  Toggle it under Skins.
- The "+", "-" and "x" of window buttons and the quest tracker are drawn
  instead of typed, so they sit centered and equally bold.
- NPC quest window: honor, skill point and reputation rewards also get
  reward cards.

## 1.1.1

- Now also published on CurseForge. No changes in the addon itself.

## 1.1.0

- Skin for the spellbook and talents window: flat panel, square spell icons
  on hover cards, icon tiles for the spell categories, and talents as square
  tiles with borders colored by state (green while points can go in, gold
  when maxed). Toggle it under Skins.
- Skin for right-click and dropdown menus: flat panel, subtle row highlight,
  flat checkboxes and radio buttons.
- Skin for the NPC quest window (greeting, details, progress, rewards) and
  the gossip window: light text on the dark panel, reward cards with
  quality borders and the chosen reward in gold.
- Pet frame card, like the target of target's; its border turns red while
  the pet attacks.
- Swing timers (main hand, off hand, ranged) get the castbar look.

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Player, target and focus frames with 3D portraits, shields, a recent-damage
  indicator, low health warning and rarity accents.
- Compact target-of-target cards for target and focus.
- Target and focus auras with debuff type borders and short countdowns.
- Restyled player, target and focus castbars with a latency marker.
- Restyled action bars, bag bar and micro menu, with options to hide the
  micro menu and bag bar.
- Skins for tooltips, the quest tracker, the loot window, bags (including
  Bagnon) and the damage meter.
- Bar texture and bar font settings.
- Settings panel with an overview page and one page per part of the UI.
